Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Stitch Genie app ("Application") for mobile devices, created by Sarthak Patel ("Service Provider"). This service is intended for use "AS IS".

Information Collection and Use

The Application collects the following information to provide its core functionality:

  • Google account information (name and email address) — collected via Google Sign-In for authentication purposes
  • Contacts (name and phone number) — accessed only when you choose to import a customer from your device's contact list, to save you re-typing it
  • Customer data (names, phone numbers, and measurements) — entered by the user to manage their tailoring business
  • Order details — entered by the user to track tailoring orders
  • Voice recordings and photos — captured when you use the app's voice-note or photo-attachment features, to help you document orders and measurements
  • AI usage data — the content you send to AI features (chat, voice transcription, order creation) is sent to the third-party AI providers listed below to generate a response

All customer, order, and measurement data is stored locally on your device. If you have multiple devices, data may also sync between them via Google Firebase/Firestore, scoped privately to your own account — no other user of the app can access it. Data is optionally also backed up to your own personal Google Drive account. The Service Provider does not have general access to this data, other than as needed to operate the AI and sync features described here.

The Application does not collect location data or usage analytics beyond what is listed above.

The Application uses Artificial Intelligence (AI) technologies to provide an assistant feature. AI processing is performed in accordance with this privacy policy. If you have questions about the AI features, please contact the Service Provider.

Third Party Access

The Service Provider does not sell, trade, or share your personal data with third parties, except as described below.

The Application uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Play Services — used for Google Sign-In authentication
  • Google Firebase — used for authentication and, if you use the app on multiple devices, to sync your business data between them
  • Google Drive API — used solely to back up and restore your data to your own Google Drive account
  • Google Gemini API — used to power the app's AI assistant, transcription, and order-creation features
  • Sarvam AI — used to power some voice transcription and text-to-speech features

The Service Provider may disclose information only:

  • as required by law, such as to comply with a subpoena or similar legal process;
  • when necessary to protect the rights, safety, or security of users or others.

Data Storage

All your data (customers, orders, measurements) is stored locally on your device using on-device storage. If you use the app on more than one device, your data also syncs through Google Firestore, in a section of the database private to your account only — no other user of the app can read or write it. If you enable backup, data is also stored in your personal Google Drive account under the app's private folder, which is only accessible to you and the Application.

Opt-Out Rights

You can stop all collection of information by uninstalling the Application. You may use the standard uninstall processes available on your mobile device or via the Google Play Store.

Data Retention Policy

Customer and order data is stored on your device (and, if you sync across devices, in Firestore) until you choose to delete it. To request deletion of your account and all associated data, visit our account deletion page or contact the Service Provider at sarthakpatel2002@gmail.com. Requests are processed within a reasonable time.

Children

The Application is not directed at children under the age of 13. The Service Provider does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 years of age. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, please contact the Service Provider at sarthakpatel2002@gmail.com.

Security

The Service Provider is committed to safeguarding your information. Data is stored locally on your device, in your personal Google Drive account, and — for multi-device sync — in Google Firestore, access-controlled so only your own account can read or write it. The Service Provider does not sell or otherwise use your data beyond operating the features described in this policy.
